How the model works
  • Availability: P(plays) for every player from the official injury report status, reason and timing.
  • Minutes: expected minutes and a distribution given he plays: role, recent minutes, teammates out, rest, blowout risk.
  • Per-minute rates: Bayesian-shrunk scoring, rebounding and playmaking rates, opponent and pace adjusted.
  • Simulation: Monte Carlo of each game prices every Kalshi market (ML, spread, total, halves, quarters, props, first basket) from one distribution.
  • Betting: edge vs Kalshi ask including fees; families need positive ROI in a strictly out-of-sample 3-season walk-forward; fractional Kelly.

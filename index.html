How the model works
  1. Availability. A play probability for every player from the official injury report status, reason and timing.
  2. Minutes. Expected minutes and a distribution if he plays: role, recent minutes, teammates out, rest, blowout risk.
  3. Per-minute rates. Bayesian-shrunk scoring, rebounding and playmaking rates, adjusted for opponent and pace.
  4. Simulation. A Monte Carlo of each game prices every Kalshi market (moneyline, spread, total, halves, quarters, props, first basket) from one distribution.
  5. Betting. Edge against the Kalshi ask including fees; a family needs positive ROI in a strictly out-of-sample three-season walk-forward before it is bet; fractional Kelly.
